# Safety Standard Operating Procedures (SOP-SAF-001)

## 1. Emergency Shutdown System (ESD)

### 1.1 Process Unit Emergency Shutdown
In the event of an uncontrolled hydrocarbon release, vapor cloud detection, or active fire within process unit boundaries, the Emergency Shutdown System (ESD) must be initiated immediately. The Level 1 ESD closes all automated unit isolation valves (XV-101 through XV-108), isolates fuel gas lines to fired heaters, and triggers emergency depressurization to the flare header. Evacuation of all non-essential personnel to designated Muster Point Charlie is mandatory within 90 seconds of ESD alarm sounding.

### 1.2 Manual ESD Overrides
Manual bypass or override of ESD interlocks is strictly prohibited during normal operations. An emergency bypass requires dual authorization: written sign-off from the Shift Superintendent and the Lead Process Safety Engineer. Any active bypass must be logged in the Safety Interlock Registry (SIR) with compensating manual monitoring stationed at the field valve location. No bypass shall exceed 4 hours without formal Management of Change (MOC) approval.

## 2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

### 2.1 Process Area Minimum PPE
All personnel entering designated operational zones must wear NFPA 2112 certified flame-resistant clothing (FRC), EN 397 industrial safety helmets with chin straps, EN 166 safety eyewear with side shields, EN ISO 20345 steel-toed antistatic safety boots, and calibrated four-gas personal atmospheric monitors (H2S, LEL, CO, O2).

### 2.2 Corrosive and Toxic Handling Protocols
Handling of amine solutions, sulfuric acid, or caustic wash chemicals requires chemical splash goggles, full face shield, heavy-duty butyl rubber gloves, and neoprene chemical aprons. An emergency eye wash and safety deluge shower station must be verified operational within 10 meters of the handling point before commencing chemical transfer.

## 3. Hot Work and Confined Space Entry

### 3.1 Hot Work Permitting
No welding, torch cutting, or spark-generating tools are permitted without a Class A Hot Work Permit. Continuous combustible gas monitoring (LEL < 1%) and an active 30-minute fire watch post-work are mandatory. Continuous fire water pressurized hose must be charged and manned on site.

### 3.2 Confined Space Entry (CSE)
Entry into process vessels, columns, or storage tanks requires atmospheric testing showing Oxygen 19.5% - 23.5%, LEL 0%, H2S < 5 ppm, and CO < 25 ppm. A dedicated Entry Attendant must maintain constant visual and radio communication and log all entrants.
