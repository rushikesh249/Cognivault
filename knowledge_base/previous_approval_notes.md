# Historical Approval Notes and Engineering Justifications (AN-HIST-012)

## 1. Approval Note AN-2024-089: Crude Preheat Exchanger Bypass Line Replacement

### 1.1 Executive Summary and Operational Context
On 14 August 2024, an engineering approval note was sanctioned to replace a 6-inch carbon steel bypass spool (Line 6-CR-104-A1A) on the crude preheat train exchanger E-102B. The spool experienced localized erosion-corrosion resulting in an actual wall thickness of 3.8 mm against a calculated minimum thickness {min}$ of 4.1 mm under operating pressure of 24.5 bar gauge.

### 1.2 Regulatory Compliance and Standards Justification
The engineering evaluation cited ASME B31.3 Section 304.1.2 calculation guidelines and Asset Integrity Guideline AIG-NDT-002 Section 1.2. The replacement material was upgraded to ASTM A312 TP316L stainless steel to provide enhanced resistance to naphthenic acid corrosion and high-velocity slurry wear. The technical justification was approved by the Plant Asset Integrity Manager and Operations Superintendent with zero safety incidents during execution.

## 2. Approval Note AN-2024-115: Amine Contactor Bottom Pump P-302B Mechanical Seal Upgrade

### 2.1 Failure Investigation and Root Cause
Recurring seal face degradation and amine leakage occurred on P-302B during Q2 2024. Failure analysis revealed vapor flashing across seal faces due to insufficient barrier fluid margin in the API Plan 52 unpressurized reservoir during hot weather ambient peaks.

### 2.2 Approved Engineering Modifications
In accordance with Maintenance Manual MOM-MEC-004 Section 1.2 and API 682 4th Edition, the seal arrangement was converted from API Plan 52 to API Plan 53A with pressurized synthetic barrier fluid maintained at 4.2 bar (1.8 bar above stuffing box pressure). The upgrade eliminated fugitive amine emissions and achieved continuous MTBF exceeding 18 months.

## 3. Approval Note AN-2025-014: Slop Oil Storage Tank TK-402 Floor Plate Repair

### 3.1 MFL Inspection Findings
API 653 out-of-service inspection conducted on Tank TK-402 identified 14 localized underside pitting indications exceeding 60% nominal bottom plate thickness (original 8 mm nominal, remaining 3.1 mm in critical zone).

### 3.2 Remediation Authorization
Pursuant to Inspection Guidelines AIG-NDT-002 Section 3.1 and API 653 Section 9.13, authorization was granted to install 6 mm ASTM A36 patch plates with continuous fillet welds and 100% vacuum box testing. Post-repair inspection confirmed zero leaks and certified the tank for 10-year extended service interval.
