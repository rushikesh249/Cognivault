# Industrial Equipment and Engineering Standards (STD-ENG-008)

## 1. Piping and Pressure Vessel Codes

### 1.1 ASME Section VIII Division 1 and 2
All unfired pressure vessels designed and fabricated for facility service must comply with ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code Section VIII Division 1 or Division 2. Vessels in sour service (H2S partial pressure > 0.05 psia) must additionally meet NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 requirements for sulfide stress cracking (SSC) resistance, including maximum base metal hardness of 22 HRC.

### 1.2 ASME B31.3 Process Piping
Process piping systems carrying flammable, toxic, or hazardous fluids are governed by ASME B31.3 Category D or Normal Fluid Service specifications. Radiographic or ultrasonic non-destructive examination is mandatory for 100% of butt welds in severe cyclic conditions and a minimum 5% random examination for Normal Fluid Service lines.

## 2. API Pump and Compressor Specifications

### 2.1 API 610 Centrifugal Pumps
Heavy-duty process pumps in hydrocarbon service must conform to API 610 (11th/12th Edition) Type OH2 (single-stage overhung centerline mounted) or Type BB2/BB3 (between-bearing multistage). Cast iron casings are strictly prohibited for flammable service; minimum casing material is ASTM A216 Grade WCB carbon steel or ASTM A351 Grade CF8M stainless steel.

### 2.2 API 618 Reciprocating Compressors
Hydrogen make-up and recycle gas reciprocating compressors must comply with API 618. Cylinder design pressure must exceed maximum settled-out pressure plus 10%. Pulsation suppression devices (dampeners) must be designed per API 618 Design Approach 3 acoustic simulation.

## 3. Electrical and Instrumentation Enclosure Ratings

### 3.1 Hazardous Area Classification (NEC Article 500 / IEC 60079)
Zone 1 / Class I Division 1 areas require explosion-proof (Ex d / Flameproof) enclosures rated for Gas Group IIB+H2 and Temperature Class T3 (200°C maximum surface temperature). Zone 2 / Class I Division 2 areas may use increased safety (Ex e) or non-incendive (Ex nA) equipment with ingress protection minimum IP66.
