# Preventive Maintenance and Overhaul Manual (MOM-MEC-004)

## 1. Centrifugal Pump Maintenance and Vibration Limits

### 1.1 Vibration Baseline and Alarm Thresholds
Centrifugal hydrocarbon pumps (P-201A/B through P-208A/B) must be monitored for vibration according to ISO 10816-3 Class II standards. Overall vibration velocity (RMS) shall not exceed 2.8 mm/s for newly overhauled units. An alert alarm is triggered at 4.5 mm/s RMS, requiring spectral analysis within 24 hours. If vibration exceeds 7.1 mm/s RMS, the pump must be immediately switched to the installed standby spare and tripped for overhaul.

### 1.2 Mechanical Seal Flush Systems
API Plan 53A and Plan 54 pressurized barrier fluid systems must maintain barrier pressure at a minimum of 1.4 bar (20 psi) above maximum seal chamber pressure. Barrier fluid reservoir levels, temperature, and nitrogen blanket pressure must be inspected daily. Any sudden loss of barrier pressure indicates primary seal face failure and requires immediate unit changeover.

## 2. Heat Exchanger and Reboiler Overhaul

### 2.1 Shell and Tube Bundle Cleaning
Bundle pulling and hydro-jetting of crude distillation preheat train exchangers (E-101 through E-106) must be performed using demineralized water at 700 to 1000 bar pressure. Tubes must be inspected for scale deposition, pitting corrosion, and wall thinning using internal rotary inspection system (IRIS) ultrasonic testing.

### 2.2 Gasket Replacement and Bolt Torquing
All channel head and floating head gaskets must be replaced with new spiral-wound 316L stainless steel gaskets with flexible graphite filler on every disassembly. Stud bolts must be lubricated with high-temperature nickel anti-seize and torqued in a cross-pattern star sequence according to Table 4.2 torque specifications (maximum 420 Nm for B7/2H 1-1/8 inch studs).

## 3. Control Valve and Actuator Calibration

### 3.1 Stroke and Hysteresis Testing
Pneumatic control valves (FV, PV, LV, TV series) must undergo annual stroke verification, linearity check, and seat leakage testing per ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class IV or Class V standards. Valve positioners (smart digital HART/Foundation Fieldbus) must be calibrated across 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% input signals with hysteresis not exceeding 0.5% of span.
